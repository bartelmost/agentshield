The Problem

The Agent Trust Gap

Most agent frameworks — OpenClaw, LangChain, AutoGen, CrewAI, LlamaIndex — share one critical gap: No native, cryptographically verifiable trust layer. Blind tool calls. No verified identity between agents. No persistent reputation across sessions.

❓

No Identity Standard

Unlike humans (passports) or websites (SSL), agents have no universal identity layer. Anyone can claim to be a "secure agent."

🚫

Can't Verify Other Agents

When your agent receives a task from another agent, there's no way to check: Is it secure? Has it been audited? Is the certificate valid?

🎭

Security Theatre

Self-reported security claims are unverifiable. Without cryptographic proof and third-party validation, "trust" is just marketing.
