About us

Our Story

The journey from a late-night discovery to a new standard in Agent Security.

The Night That Changed Everything

It started with a realization. While the world was rushing to deploy LLM-powered agents, a massive gap was opening up: Security.

Johannes Hofmann, with 23 years of experience in the security sector, spent weeks exploring the boundaries of autonomous AI agents across multiple platforms. During one long night of testing, the "Discovery Story" of AgentShield began. It wasn't just about finding bugs; it was about the realization that agents are the most vulnerable and most powerful tools we've ever built.

That night, "AgentShield" was born. We made the night into day, coding the first prototype to ensure that the AI agents of tomorrow are as secure as the cloud infrastructure of today.

Vision

Our goal is simple: Make Agent Security the Standard.

We believe no agent should be deployed without a safety net. Whether it's prompt injection protection or token optimization to save costs, AgentShield provides the technical authority to scale AI safely.
